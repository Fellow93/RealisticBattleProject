# RBMAI — deep water nav blocker, walls, forced exit, slot/steering patches

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-30. Keep adding design notes for this area here, not in CLAUDE.md.

- `AiModule/Terrain/DeepWaterNavBlocker.cs` — Deep water blocks AI paths (`deepWaterNavBlockEnabled`, default ON, XML `/Config/RBMAI/DeepWaterNavBlockEnabled`; its log `deepWaterNavLoggingEnabled` default off; MissionLogic added in `RBM/SubModule.cs` under `rbmAiEnabled`): in land field battles only (`Mission.IsFieldBattle`, custom battles included; siege/sally/naval/raid/NoTeamAI skipped) its `EarlyStart` — main thread, after `MissionCombatantsLogic.EarlyStart` sets the team-AI type, before any spawn or AI tick — makes swim-deep water a no-go area for EVERYONE (USER RULE 2026-10-20, final: a hard wall "like a big rock", the player included; missiles and the camera pass), because disabling faces alone only changes path searches (first log) and an AILimiter wall did not hold shoved men (second log: 147 AI men inside): (1) an immutable `DeepWaterGrid` (≤1 m step over the navmesh bbox ∩ `Mission.Boundaries`, ≤ 600k cells; deep = water − terrain ≥ `WallDepth` 1.0 m (user 2026-10-20; was 1.3, the swamp floor at 1.30–1.31 m flickered across it), swim starts at 1.34 m; bridge = `GetHeightAtPoint` solid surface AND `WorldPosition.GetNavMeshZ` at the water line; deep regions and dry holes < 16 cells removed), published through a volatile field; (2) a DOUBLE wall along the shoreline as a SMOOTH CURVE (user 2026-10-20: "a bit rounder so AI would not get stuck on them but at least slide a bit"; it used to follow the 1 m cell edges, a staircase whose inner corners snagged men): `BuildWallCurves` runs marching squares over the deep grid (outside the grid = not deep, so every curve closes; saddles resolved DEEP-CONNECTED, matching the 4-connected dry flood in `GridConnected`), chains the pieces into polylines with deep water on the left (orientation checked by sampling, flipped if wrong), smooths with 5 Laplacian (0.5/0.25/0.25) + 2 Chaikin passes (corners ≈ 2–3 m radius), simplifies with Douglas–Peucker 0.15 m, and moves the curve 0.4 m (`WallDeepBias`) along its vertex normals toward deep water, pushing any vertex still in a dry cell up to 0.6 m further, so the wall never cuts into dry cells; legs are split to ≤ 3 m quads (height from the terrain along each quad). Outer quad on the curve, inner one 0.3 m (`WallLayerGap`) further in (same normals, so it closes too); consecutive quads share end points exactly, with a 0.1 m overlap only where neighbours fall in different pieces; both layers of a leg in the same piece. Parts of deep shore cells now lie OUTSIDE the wall: `InsideWall` = deep cell AND enclosed by the outer curve (even-odd ray test over the exact, unoverlapped curve, 2 m y-bands) drives DEEPCELL entries, the forced-exit scan (men outside are never forced, counted in END) and the gate's "still inside" test; WALL logs polylines, raw/smoothed/simplified points, pushes, shortest quad and ms; one entity per 6 m tile ("piece"): `GameEntity.CreateEmpty` + `ReplacePhysicsBodyWithQuadPhysicsBody` (pinned buffers, the NavalDLC `ShipAttachmentMachine` pattern) with `Moveable | AgentOnly | TwoSided` — AgentOnly (0x200000) is not in `CommonCollisionExcludeFlagsForAgent` but is in the missile/camera excludes; `Remove(78)` on revert/mission end. FORCED EXIT (⚠️ USER RULE 2026-10-20, new: "they should simply not be there", "forced to swim out" — overrides, for THIS feature only, the older "no scripted swim-to-shore rescue" rule; the old 4–12 s gate let ~25 men follow one leaker in, fourth log): every 0.5 s each AI man (never the player) standing or swimming in a deep cell, ≤ 80 at once, gets an exit point — BFS from his cell through blocked cells to the nearest non-blocked cells, those with an enabled navmesh face compared (one per distinct gate, +4 m when a man is close outside it, gates that failed for him skipped) — and is driven there with `Agent.SetScriptedPosition` (GoToPosition; re-issued when the flag is gone, checked every 0.5 s); released with `DisableScriptedMovement` the moment he stands in a non-blocked cell, on player control, revert and mission end (on removal only the tracking is dropped, no native call). Evidence it works for swimmers: NavalDLC `AgentNavalAIComponent.OnTick` (naval raid overboard men) does exactly this while `IsInWater()` and releases on `IsOnLand()`; native `LadderQueueManager` scripts formation members the same way. His gate = the piece(s) his line to the exit crosses in its last 3.5 m; it opens (`SetBodyFlags(BodyFlag | BodyFlags.Disabled)`, how `CastleGate` toggles its extra door colliders and NavalDLC its plank bridge; closed by clearing Disabled + `SetFrameChanged`; was `SetPhysicsState`, both layers) ONLY while he is in a deep cell within 0.6 m of it, closes once he is out of the deep cell and 0.6 m clear, or after 2 s at most (1 s reopen cooldown, ≤ 20 open), and waits while any man outside (player too) is within 1.5 m of it moving toward it or leaning on it (≤ 0.6 m); after 2 s waiting he is re-routed, and once he has waited 6 s in all it opens anyway (outsiders pressing along a bad path never clear). DONE lines split the time: to the gate / waited / first opening to out. Piece entities sit at their piece (frame origin = centre, lowest bottom; vertices local, the NavalDLC pattern; was identity frame + world vertices) with `SetFrameChanged` + `SetManualLocalBoundingBox`; WALL logs triangles and whether each entity's global box covers its wall, WALLTEST ray-casts across 24 sample pieces (first tick, every 60 s), WALLLEAK describes each closed piece's first crossing (quads near the move with layer/ends/z/where it crossed, terrain/water, physics state/flags/triangles/bounds, ray casts along the move at knee and chest height, slot + `CanMoveDirectlyToPosition`, face under him) — the 16:09 log had 547 crossings of closed pieces, 112 of the logged 182 through pieces never opened. An attempt fails after 30 s or 4 openings and is retried through another piece. A Frontline pin is a movement lock (`SetTargetPosition`), so it is cleared at start and Frontline's `GetOrderPositionOfUnitCore` skips these men via `DeepWaterSlots.IsForcedExit(agent.Index)` (a `ConcurrentDictionary<int, byte>` + Interlocked count, main-thread writes, worker reads; cleared in `MissionStartReset` through `DeepWaterSlots.Clear()`). Its `IsOnLand` swimmer gate did not cover men wading at 1.0–1.34 m; (3) no slots in deep water: `AiModule/Terrain/DeepWaterSlotPatches.cs` postfixes `Formation`'s explicit `IFormation.BatchUnitPositions` (the native slot batch every LineFormation-derived arrangement uses, which never consults the additional condition — the main leak; ⚠️ not `Mission.BatchFormationUnitPositions`: that one-line internal wrapper is JIT-inlined, so a patch on it never ran) and turns availability 2 (available) into 1 (unavailable) for slots in blocked cells; it reads the static `DeepWaterSlots.LiveGrid` (set/cleared by the blocker, cleared in `MissionStartReset`), as does a one-line gate in Frontline's `IsPositionOccupied` (pins never land in deep water); plus a `Mission.IsFormationUnitPositionAvailable_AdditionalCondition` subscriber for single-slot / order-position checks — ⚠️ both run on WORKER threads and read only the grid (no engine call, no allocation, Interlocked counters); multicast Func, last subscriber wins (native subscribes only in `SiegeDeploymentHandler`). Left open: ColumnFormation (no slot table), Charge targets, OrderReactionHold's earlier (filtered) slots. Faces are switched off for paths when their centre is ≥ `BlockDepth` (1.0 m, kept in step with `WallDepth`) or in a deep cell, or (option #1, user 2026-10-20 "units should try to navigate more around those walls") when ≥ `FaceDeepShareBlock` 0.25 of their sampled area is deep: `ComputeFaceDeepShares` maps grid cells within 40 m (Chebyshev) of deep cells to faces with `GetTerrainHeight` + `GetNavMeshFaceIndex` (Vec3, terrain + 0.3, checkIfDisabled false), stride raised so ≤ 120k samples; logged as BLOCK faceShare / deepShare histogram. The first-tick check also reverts when > 50% of a side stands on faces we switched off. NOT a path: native `HumanAIComponent.ParallelUpdateFormationMovement` passes every man within ~22 m (2 × 3 × formation speed) of his place in the MOVING block (`Formation.GetCurrentGlobalPositionOfUnit` = `CurrentPosition` + local offset, never slot-filtered) to `Agent.SetFormationIntegrityData`, and the engine steers him straight there — a block sweeping across a swamp walks men at the wall (16:09 log: 547 crossings, all Move, slot dry). RBM's own Move-order postfix (`Frontline/FormationMovement.cs` `OverrideParallelFormationMovement`) calls it again for every AI man. Fixed (user-approved 2026-10-20) by `DeepWaterSteering` in `DeepWaterSlotPatches.cs`: a TRANSPILER on `HumanAIComponent.ParallelUpdateFormationMovement` swaps both native `callvirt Agent.SetFormationIntegrityData` for `call DeepWaterSteering.SetFormationIntegrityData(agent, …)` (same stack), and RBM's postfix calls the helper directly (one call-site swap); when the target lies in a blocked cell or the straight line to it crosses a deep cell, position becomes `Vec2.Zero` (native's "not catching up"), so he follows his path-searched formation frame. Not a prefix on `Agent.SetFormationIntegrityData`: a ~29-byte IL wrapper the JIT may inline (the `Mission.BatchFormationUnitPositions` lesson). Worker thread: grid reads, `Agent.Position` (pointer read), Interlocked `Calls`/`Nulled` (END, with `nativeCallSitesTranspiled`, expect 2). WINDING: `BodyFlags.TwoSided` did not make the quads two-sided — the 16:59 log's WALLTEST hit 10/24 pieces (dry → deep rays) and all 27 quads crossed in its WALLLEAK lines were crossed along their (A_top, B_top, B_bottom) normal, i.e. through the back face (that normal is fixed by the run direction, the deep side is either way, so ~half the wall was open from the dry side); every quad now has both windings (12 indices, triangles = 4 × quads). The mesh-less entities' bounds were a point at the origin; now set with `SetManualLocalBoundingBox` (the NavalDLC `ShipOarMachine`/`ShipUnmannedOar`/`RopePileBaked` call; `SetManualGlobalBoundingBox` as a logged fallback), re-applied after each gate close; ray hits on 10 point-box pieces show the box was not the leak. WALLTEST now casts both ways (dry → deep and deep → dry). `SetAbilityOfFaceWithIndex` returns the face's enabled state BEFORE the call (disable → true = ours; re-enable → false = undone); only the last call passes `updateIslandIndices: true` (enough, per the log). REVERT (faces, walls, slot condition together) on any failure or when the first-tick check says the sides are cut apart: `DoesPathExistBetweenFaces` (ignoreDisabled false AND true) plus a 4-connected flood fill over the grid's non-deep cells (the walls' own connectivity, which the face check cannot see). Log (`deepWaterNavLoggingEnabled`, Debug & Logging, XML `/Config/RBMAI/DeepWaterNavLoggingEnabled`) to `logs/deepwater/`, one file per battle via `BufferedLogWriter`/`LogRetention`: BLOCK, GRID, WALL, SLOTS, PASS, CONNECTIVITY, REVERTED, SWIM (with inDeepCell + own slot deep/margin/dry, raw movement mode — `IsOnLand` is mode & 3 == Land, so None (airborne?) reads as "in water" — isInWater, height above ground, terrain heightmap and the other water-query variants), DEEPCELL (via OPEN_GATE / CLOSED_PIECE / NO_WALL_CROSSED, positions 0.25 s and 1 s earlier, speed, friends behind him), FORCEDEXIT (START/DONE/FAILED/REROUTE/NOEXIT/GAVEUP/RELEASED/REMOVED), GATE (OPEN/CLOSE/WAIT, physics state read back), WATCH, STUCK, WALLSTUCK, END (incl. WALLSTUCK episodes with a dry slot — the measure for the unbuilt "option #1", blocking faces with a large deep share); the player is labelled PLAYER, counted in DEEPCELL, kept out of the AI stuck diagnostics. Swimmers outside deep cells are only logged, never moved. All per-agent state is instance fields. Debug view: `AiModule/Terrain/NavigableDebugOverlay.cs` (+ prefab `RBMXML/GUI/Prefabs/RBMNavigableDebug.xml`), a MissionLogic added under `rbmAiEnabled` when `deepWaterNavBlockEnabled || deepWaterNavLoggingEnabled` (so it also previews with the blocker off), inert (no layer, no query) until Ctrl+Shift+W, which toggles it on/off: a 2 m grid, 40 m radius, ≤ 1500 points around the looked-at ground point, re-sampled on a cell change or every 0.5 s, dots by `WorldToScreen` every frame. Classes from the blocker's grid / `GetNavMeshFaceIndex` (Vec3) with checkIfDisabled false/true + the blocker's internal read-only state (`Grid`, `IsWallLive`, `WallSegments`, `IsFaceDisabledByUs`, `BlockResult`, `IsPlausible`, `ProbeCell`): red deep water (live grid: walled off, no slots), magenta would be deep (grid after a revert, or `ProbeCell` itself when the blocker is off — the one shared rule), dark red face off for paths (ours, not deep), orange blocked by the scene, cyan navigable but wet, green navigable, white dots along the live wall segments, both layers (yellow = piece open as a gate), a big blue dot per man under forced exit and a small pale-blue one at his exit point (`CopyForcedExitMarkers`, every frame), no navmesh not drawn. Readout: blocker status, grid step/deep cells, wall quads/entities, slot rejections, class counts, engine calls/ms per re-sample, a "query check" (our faces returned by checkIfDisabled=true, the two calls disagreeing — verifies the undocumented flag in game), and the point under the screen centre.
