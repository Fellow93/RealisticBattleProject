# Realistic Battle Mod

**Realistic Battle Mod (RBM)** is a combat and campaign overhaul for *Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord*.
It changes how blows deal damage and how armor stops them, how battle AI fights in formations,
adds a posture and stamina system, and reworks the campaign economy around troop spoils, wages and
settlement wealth.

Every part of the mod can be switched on or off separately, so you can use only the parts you want.

## Where to start

- **New to RBM?** Read [Getting Started](getting-started.md) for installation, load order and compatibility.
- **Want to know what changed?** The Features section covers each part of the mod:
    - [Combat & Armor](features/combat.md): damage, armor, penetration and blunt trauma
    - [Ranged Combat](features/ranged.md): bows, crossbows, throwing weapons and ammunition
    - [Posture & Stamina](features/posture.md): blocking, guard breaks and fatigue
    - [Battle AI](features/ai.md): formations, frontlines and tactics
    - [Campaign](features/campaign.md): spoils, wages, upgrades and the settlement economy
    - [Tournaments](features/tournament.md): tournament participants, simulation and prizes
- **Tuning the mod?** Every option on the in-game settings screen is explained in the [Settings Reference](settings.md).
